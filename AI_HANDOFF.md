## Objective

## Third-stop light meter (2026-08-09, uncommitted)

The fifth pass recorded that quantising the meter would be a product decision, not an optimization. The user then made that decision: the meter now reads in third-stops. **Implemented by Codex CLI** (`gpt-5.6-sol`, effort low, via `agent-delegation-tools/codex.ps1`); reviewed and verified in the Claude session, because Codex's sandbox had no Node on its `PATH` and could not run any of the three gates itself.

- `app/viewport.tsx` gained `const METER_STEP_EV = 1 / 3` and the `lightDue` branch now reports `Math.round(light.ev / METER_STEP_EV) * METER_STEP_EV`, replacing the raw `Math.abs(light.ev - lastLightEv) >= 0.025` threshold. The quantised value is what React receives — the raw EV is not retained anywhere.
- **Quantisation is consistent all the way down, on purpose.** `sceneLightEv`, `targetEv`, the `effective` solve, `exposureDelta`, `brightness` and the capture score all read the same third-stop value. Because both sides of `exposureDelta = sceneLightEv - cameraEv` use it, a correctly-set camera still scores full marks; the meter's resolution is not turned into an uncorrectable scoring penalty. The alternative — solving from a quantised reading but exposing the photograph from a hidden precise EV — was considered and rejected for that reason.
- **Visible consequence, accepted:** a captured photo's brightness can differ by at most 1/6 EV (a factor of 1.122) from what the old continuous meter would have produced, and it now always agrees with the settings shown in the HUD. This is a deliberate behaviour change and the first one in this optimization sequence.
- Arithmetic verified directly in Node: the expression is bit-stable across 200,000 repeated evaluations (so `!==` cannot report spuriously), every quantised value is a fixed point under re-quantisation (no drift), and the worst-case error over a −4…+4 EV sweep is exactly 0.1667 EV. Modelled against a 10 s pan sampled at the 160 ms meter cadence, reports fall from 57 to 33 (−42%); with the camera held still the meter now reports nothing at all until the metered stop actually changes.
